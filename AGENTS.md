## Public repo

This repo is public: anyone on the internet can read its files, issues, PRs, and commit history, and a deleted line stays in history. Write everything here, including issue and PR text, for a stranger to read. Keep it to what the org-wide templates, workflows, and profile need.

Leave out PSKs, tokens, and other secrets; Device IDs, customer data, and location data; internal hostnames, IPs, and GCP project IDs; and unreleased product or business plans. When a change needs that detail, put it in the private repo it concerns and refer to it here only in general terms. If you find such material already here, stop and tell the person rather than removing it yourself, since history still holds it.
